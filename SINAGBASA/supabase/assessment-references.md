# Private assessment references

## Gates-MacGinitie resources

1. **Riverside Insights — Gates-MacGinitie Reading Tests, Fourth Edition**
   https://store.riversideinsights.com/shop/gates-macginitie-reading-tests-fourth-edition
   Official publisher catalogue describing the assessment and listing administration materials, scoring keys, and norms. The listed contemporary forms are S and T. It does not provide a matching official answer key for the supplied D–Form 2 document.

2. **Riverside Insights — Fourth Edition test levels and testing times**
   https://riversideinsights.com/hubfs/GMRT4_Test_Levels_Times.pdf?hsLang=en
   Official chart identifying levels, forms, question counts, and administration times for Fourth Edition. These differ from the supplied 30-item document. Do not substitute this chart's item counts or timing into the user's assessment or describe the application's 60-minute default as publisher-prescribed.

3. **Elliott, Robert C. (December 1975), Comparative Value of a Systematic Diagnostic and Prescriptive Approach to the Teaching of Reading (Instructional Management System)**
   https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED119136.pdf
   Historical dissertation archived by ERIC. The printed page 85 entry describes the 1965 Gates-MacGinitie edition, Level D, and the three components Speed and Accuracy, Vocabulary, and Comprehension. It provides historical context rather than an item-by-item answer key for the user's supplied document. Its grade/administration information must not be transferred to the user's version without confirming the edition and form.

4. **Riverside Insights — Scoring Services Guide**
   https://info.riversideinsights.com/datamanager-onlinehelp/riverside-insights-scoring-services-guide
   Official scoring-services guidance and publisher contact route. Ask about the precise edition, Survey/Level D, Form 2, and the original matching manual/key when seeking official verification.
